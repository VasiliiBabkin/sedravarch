# Limitations

- The archive is an experiment with a 64 MiB decoded budget. The CLI reads the full input and full archive into memory, and extraction scans the entire encoded archive for CRC and record metadata. Only the requested file and its dependencies are reconstructed. The format does not store names, paths or timestamps.
- The encoder lacks intra-file LZ parsing. Fixed lags approximate arithmetic and periodic sequences but neither predict general context nor model record types. Inter-file prediction is represented by copy/add; position and value streams are interleaved in v1.
- Fixed-grid segmentation is sensitive to shifts. Short blocks below 16 bytes generate no rolling fingerprints. The sketch may miss neighbors, including exact duplicates outside the history window. Ranking and budget are deterministic heuristics. References are limited to completed earlier files.
- The delta finder keeps the first position for each exact seed and uses greedy extension. It does not solve optimal parsing or hash-table allocation. The global constrained graph optimizer and exact oracle remain specifications.
- The synthetic numerical and random cases exercise particular distributions. The single synthetic version family cannot establish a deployment-level win. Selection of parameters on a real validation collection and testing on independent families remain open.
- There is no structure transform in the archive wire format. The structural probes do not disassemble instructions, parse PDF objects or predict SQLite records. Stream detection is confined to the checked diagnostic inputs and bounded PNG/ZIP framing. BCJ2, SADM-native x86 transforms, DLL coverage and broader packed executable coverage remain open.
- The mutation campaign is a bounded deterministic check. Coverage-guided fuzzing, sanitizer runs and an independent security audit remain open. CRC-32 does not authenticate maliciously changed data.
- The shared execution environment covered local tests. No VDS run, hardware counter measurements, SIMD claims or parallel speedups are included.
